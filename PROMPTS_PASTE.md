# Archetype art: paste-ready prompts

Copy one block at a time into the Gemini app. Each block is the approved
subject paragraph from `ART_BRIEFS.md` with the shared style block already
merged in.

**Order:** generate **Warrior first** and iterate until you're happy with it.
It's the style anchor. For the other five, attach the approved Warrior image
and paste the **reference line** below *before* that archetype's block.

**Saving:** download the full-size image and save it as
`art-raw/<id>.png` (or `.jpg`): `warrior`, `vanguard`, `ninja`, `titan`,
`swordsman`, `monk`. Then run `npm run art` (see `scripts/convert-art.mjs`).

**Before converting:** do the IP review checklist at the end of `ART_BRIEFS.md`.

---

## Reference line (for archetypes 2–6, with the approved Warrior image attached)

```text
Use the attached image only as a style reference. Match its art style, line weight, cel shading, orange-to-gold rim lighting, solid near-black background and full-body framing exactly. Do not copy the reference character's face, hair, build, pose or outfit. Draw a completely different character, described below.
```

## 1. Warrior (`warrior`), generate first

```text
A lean, athletic martial artist with a pronounced V-taper: broad shoulders, wide back, narrow waist and visible abs. He has short, neatly cropped dark hair lying flat, and a determined, focused expression. He wears plain grey training wear: a charcoal-grey sleeveless training top and loose dark-grey training trousers, with off-white cloth hand wraps and bare feet. Dynamic fighting stance: weight on the front foot, lead fist raised at chin height, rear fist guarding the ribs, as if about to step forward. Faint diagonal speed lines in the dark background.

Original character illustration in a modern anime-inspired cel-shaded style: clean, confident ink lineart, two-tone cel shading with a single soft highlight, and grounded athletic anatomy (defined but believable muscle, not exaggerated). The character is a clearly adult man in his mid-twenties. Full body, centred, turned three-quarters toward the viewer, the whole figure inside the frame with the head near the top tenth and the feet near the bottom tenth; camera at chest height. The background is solid near-black (#0A0A0F) filling the entire canvas edge to edge; any environment detail described above is a faint, low-contrast silhouette that fades into the black. Lighting: a strong warm rim light from behind in energy orange (#FF6B1A) blending to gold (#FFB020) that outlines the whole silhouette, plus a soft cool front fill so the face and body stay readable. Clothing stays in muted neutrals (charcoal, slate, stone, black, oatmeal) so the rim light is the only saturated colour. Portrait 3:4 composition. No text, letters, logos, emblems, kanji, symbols, watermarks, signature, frame or border. Safe for work.
```
